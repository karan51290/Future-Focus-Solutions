Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

The information provided on this website is intended for general informational purposes only and should not be considered personalised financial, investment, legal, tax or insurance advice. Financial decisions should be made after considering individual circumstances, objectives and requirements. All financial products and services are subject to their respective terms, conditions, eligibility criteria and applicable risks.

What information we collect

When you contact us through the form on our Contact page, we collect the following information:

  • • Your name
  • • Your phone number
  • • The service you're interested in (Financial Planning, Mutual Funds, Insurance, Stocks or Commodities - Market Insights, or Other Enquiry)
  • • Your message

We also collect basic, anonymous data about how you use the website - such as which pages you visit and how long you spend on them - to help us understand how people use Future Focus Solutions and to make the site work better. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or ad-tracking services.

How your information is used

When you submit the Contact form, your information is sent directly from your own WhatsApp account to ours (via a link to https://wa.me/). The data is not stored on our website servers - it lives only in your WhatsApp conversation with us. We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, understand what financial service you need, and get in touch with you about how we can help.

How your information is shared

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

If you decide to pursue a financial product (an insurance policy or an investment) through us, we may share relevant details about you with the specific insurer or financial institution whose product you're interested in - only as needed to process your application or arrange the service. You will always be aware of this sharing and will have consented to it as part of the application process.

Data retention

We keep the information you share with us only for as long as it takes to respond to your enquiry and help you with the financial service you asked about. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so. We may retain information longer if required by Indian law or for legal and compliance reasons.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us to access, correct, or delete any personal information you've shared with us. You can make this request at any time by contacting support@futurefocussolutions.in. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

Cookies

The Future Focus Solutions website may use minimal cookies or local storage (such as your preferred language or whether you've seen a notification) for basic functionality and to improve your experience. These are not invasive tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party ad-tracking cookies. Most browsers allow you to control or delete cookies if you prefer.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always show the most recent revision. If we make a significant change, we'll make a reasonable effort to notify you.
