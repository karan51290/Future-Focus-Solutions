Real Estate

Buy, sell, or invest - with someone who's also looking at your whole financial plan.

Most real estate advice comes from someone whose only interest is closing the deal. Because we're also your financial planning team, we're looking at how a property purchase affects your loan capacity, your tax position, and your long-term goals - not just the transaction in front of us.

Residential real estate

  • • Personalized property search based on your budget, needs, and location
  • • Negotiation support to get fair value, whether you're buying or selling
  • • Extra guidance if you're a first-time buyer navigating the process for the first time

Commercial real estate

  • • Help finding the right commercial space to lease for your business
  • • Investment opportunities in commercial property, evaluated for actual return potential, not just square footage
  • • Strategies to increase the value of property you already own

Real estate as part of your plan

Buying property is rarely just a real estate decision - it affects your loan eligibility, your tax filing, and your liquidity for years afterward. We look at the purchase in that context, not in isolation.
