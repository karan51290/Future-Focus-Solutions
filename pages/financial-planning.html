Financial Planning

One plan for your income, savings, investments, and goals - not a different answer from every product you ask about.

Most "financial planning" you'll encounter is really just investment selling with extra steps. Ours starts with a conversation about what you're actually trying to do - buy a home, retire comfortably, fund your children's education, protect your family if something happens to you - and works backward from there. Some of what we recommend won't be a product we sell; that's fine. The plan is the product.

Retirement planning

  • • A clear picture of where you stand today
  • • A realistic estimate of what retirement will cost you
  • • A savings plan built to close that gap
  • • Tax-efficient structuring along the way
  • • Investments chosen to match your retirement timeline, not general market trends

Investment & wealth management

  • • Portfolios built around your goals and risk tolerance, not a model portfolio applied to everyone
  • • Ongoing risk management as markets and your circumstances change
  • • Regular reviews and rebalancing - not a "set and forget" plan
  • • Tax-aware investment choices
  • • Reporting you can actually read

Child education planning

  • • A realistic estimate of future education costs, in India or abroad
  • • Savings plans matched to your timeline
  • • A funding strategy that doesn't derail your other goals
  • • Guidance on financial aid and scholarship options where relevant

Risk & insurance planning

  • • Life insurance sized to actually replace your income, not a round number
  • • Disability and critical illness cover
  • • Long-term care planning
  • • Property and liability cover for what you own

Estate & legacy planning

  • • A clear plan for who gets what, without ambiguity or family disputes
  • • Nomination and succession documentation done properly
  • • Coordination with your insurance and investments so your estate plan doesn't contradict them
