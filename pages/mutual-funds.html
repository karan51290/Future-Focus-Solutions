Mutual Funds

Fund selection matched to your goals and timeline - not whatever's topping this month's rankings.

Most people either avoid mutual funds because the sheer number of them is overwhelming, or they pick off a "top 10 funds" list without really knowing what they own or why. We start with what you're investing for and how long you have, then build a fund selection around that - and we can explain, in plain language, why every fund in your portfolio is there.

Equity funds

  • • Large-cap, mid-cap, small-cap, and flexi-cap funds explained in plain terms, not jargon
  • • A mix chosen for goals that are genuinely long-term, where you have time to ride out the ups and downs
  • • A clear picture of the volatility you're signing up for, before you invest - not after a bad quarter
  • • Fund selection based on consistency and process, not last year's chart-topping return

Debt & hybrid funds

  • • Lower-volatility funds for goals that are a few years out, not decades
  • • A hybrid allocation that balances equity growth with debt stability, sized to your risk tolerance
  • • An honest comparison against fixed deposits and other low-risk options, so you know what you're trading off

Tax-saving (ELSS) funds

  • • Section 80C tax benefit on your investment, up to the applicable limit
  • • The shortest lock-in among 80C options - but still a real equity investment, not a savings account
  • • Fund selection on the same criteria as any equity fund - not just "it saves tax, so it'll do"

SIPs and ongoing review

  • • Help setting up a Systematic Investment Plan sized to what you can actually keep up, month after month
  • • Lump sum investing when that fits your situation better than a SIP
  • • Regular portfolio reviews, so a fund that's drifted from its purpose gets flagged, not ignored
  • • Straight advice on when to switch or exit a fund - and when to just stay the course
