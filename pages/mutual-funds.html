Mutual Funds

Investing with purpose.

Mutual funds provide access to a range of investment options designed for different financial goals, investment horizons and risk preferences. We help investors understand mutual fund options and explore a goal-oriented investment approach.

Every Investment Should Have a Purpose.

Before investing, it is important to understand what you are investing for. Your financial goals, investment horizon and risk profile are important considerations when building an investment approach. We help investors understand areas such as:

  • •Goal-based investing
  • •Systematic Investment Plans
  • •Lump-sum investments
  • •Different categories of mutual funds
  • •Portfolio organisation
  • •Periodic portfolio reviews

Building an Investment Habit.

A Systematic Investment Plan, commonly known as a SIP, allows investors to invest a chosen amount regularly in a mutual fund scheme. Instead of investing a large amount at one time, investors can contribute regularly at chosen intervals, such as monthly.

Invest Regularly

SIPs can help create a disciplined investment habit by encouraging regular contributions towards your financial goals.

Start with a Comfortable Amount

The investment amount can be selected based on your financial circumstances and investment objectives. A sustainable investment approach can often be easier to maintain over the long term.

Work Towards Long-Term Goals

SIPs may be considered as part of a long-term investment approach towards goals such as:

  • •Retirement
  • •Children's education
  • •Long-term wealth creation
  • •Other important financial objectives

Stay Focused Through Market Movements

Financial markets can experience periods of growth and decline. A disciplined investment approach can help investors remain focused on their long-term objectives rather than reacting emotionally to short-term market movements.

A SIP does not eliminate market risk or guarantee returns. Investment outcomes will depend on factors including market performance, investment duration and the nature of the selected mutual fund.

Investing Based on Your Circumstances.

In some situations, investors may consider investing a larger amount at one time. Whether a SIP or lump-sum approach is appropriate depends on several factors, including:

  • •Financial objectives
  • •Available investment amount
  • •Investment horizon
  • •Risk profile
  • •Existing investment portfolio

Understanding these factors can help investors make more informed decisions.

Understanding Mutual Fund Options

Mutual funds are available across different categories and investment approaches. Depending on individual requirements, investors may explore categories such as:

Equity Funds

Primarily invest in equity and equity-related securities and may experience significant market fluctuations.

Debt Funds

Invest in debt and money-market-related instruments and may have different risk characteristics depending on the underlying investments.

Hybrid Funds

Invest across a combination of asset classes, such as equity and debt.

Periodic Portfolio Review

Financial circumstances and priorities can change over time. Periodic reviews can help investors assess whether their investment portfolio continues to remain relevant to their current financial goals and circumstances.

Important Disclaimer

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Please read all scheme-related documents carefully before investing.

The value of investments may rise or fall depending on market conditions. Past performance is not indicative of future performance, and there is no assurance or guarantee regarding future returns.

The suitability of any mutual fund investment depends on an investor's individual financial circumstances, investment objectives, investment horizon and risk profile.
