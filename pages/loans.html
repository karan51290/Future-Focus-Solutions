What you'll need

General documents required for most loan applications in India. Exact requirements vary by loan type and lender - we'll confirm the specific list for your application during your consultation.

  • ✓ Identity proof - Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Voter ID
  • ✓ Address proof - Aadhaar Card, recent utility bill, or Passport
  • ✓ Income proof - latest salary slips and Form 16 for salaried applicants; ITR and audited financials for self-employed and business applicants
  • ✓ Bank statements for the last 6 months
  • ✓ Passport-size photographs
  • ✓ Property documents, for home loans
  • ✓ Business registration and financial statements, for business loans
