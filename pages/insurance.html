Insurance

Cover for your health, your life, your vehicle, your home, and your business - matched to what you actually need.

Insurance is where people most often end up either over-insured on things that don't matter or under-insured on the one thing that would actually hurt. We walk through your situation and help you figure out what's worth paying for.

Life Insurance

Financial protection for your family if something happens to you.

  • Term life insurance - affordable cover that protects your family financially for a defined period
  • Whole life insurance - lifetime protection with a savings component built in
  • Unit Linked Insurance Plans (ULIPs) - life cover combined with investment, for those who understand and want that trade-off

General Insurance

Everything that isn't life cover - your health, vehicle, travel, home, and business.

Health Insurance

  • Individual health insurance - coverage for your medical expenses, so a hospital bill doesn't become a financial setback
  • Family health insurance - one plan covering medical costs for your entire family
  • Critical illness insurance - a lump sum on diagnosis of specified illnesses, to cover costs your regular health policy won't

Auto Insurance

  • Third-party liability insurance - legally mandatory cover for damage or injury caused to others
  • Comprehensive car insurance - protection against accidents, theft, and non-collision damage like flooding or fire
  • Two-wheeler insurance - cover for your bike or scooter against theft and accidents

Travel Insurance

  • Domestic travel insurance - medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and baggage loss within India
  • International travel insurance - medical expenses, trip interruptions, and lost luggage abroad
  • Student travel insurance - cover for students studying overseas

Home Insurance

  • Homeowners insurance - protection for your home, belongings, and liability

Property Insurance

  • Commercial property insurance - cover for your business's buildings, equipment, and inventory

Commercial Vehicle Insurance

  • Cover for vehicles used for business purposes, including damage and third-party liability

How to choose the right cover

The honest answer is usually: enough term life insurance to replace your income for your dependents' sake, a health policy that covers your actual likely hospital costs (not the cheapest premium), and cover for anything you couldn't afford to replace out of pocket.
