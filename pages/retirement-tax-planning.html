Retirement & Tax Planning

Build the income you'll need later, and keep more of what you earn now.

Retirement planning and tax planning are usually sold as separate services, but the decisions overlap constantly - how you save for retirement changes your tax bill, and how you plan your taxes changes how fast you can save. We handle both together so one doesn't undercut the other.

Retirement planning

  • • Retirement income strategy - a plan for steady income through your retirement years, not just a lump sum with no drawdown plan
  • • Pension planning - understanding what your existing pension plans actually pay out, and where the gaps are

Investment planning for retirement

  • • Portfolio allocation that shifts as retirement gets closer
  • • Risk assessment specific to a retirement timeline, not a general-purpose portfolio
  • • Estate planning that coordinates with your retirement assets, not a separate document that contradicts them

Tax planning

  • • Tax optimization strategies under current Indian tax law
  • • Tax-efficient investment choices - ELSS, PPF, NPS, and other Section 80C/80D options weighed against your actual situation
  • • Help preparing and filing your returns accurately and on time

Healthcare & long-term care planning

  • • Health insurance and top-up cover sized for rising medical costs in retirement
  • • Critical illness cover
  • • Long-term care planning, so a health event doesn't force you to liquidate assets you were counting on

The earlier you plan retirement and tax together, the more options you have.
